Turning adjusts the clock, never the presets below. Once it is off its preset the label reads from 20m, and a double tap or a hold puts it back. Nothing but a tap or a press starts it.

Neither the double press nor the hold starts it. Every gesture is acknowledged on the key itself — the ring pulses and the line under the clock names what just happened.

Presets

Click a dot to load that preset onto the control. Holding the screenkey cycles forward through this list one at a time; here you can go straight to any of them.

    A preset can have several steps. Type them separated by commas and the timer runs them one after another, with no gap — 40, 10, 10 is forty minutes, then ten, then ten. The line under the clock counts them off as ×2/3.

    A bare number means minutes. Say 90s or 1h30m for anything else, and 10m x3 to repeat a step. Whatever you type, the grey line underneath says what it was read as before you leave the row.

    Appearance

    Name the timer and the line under the clock says it — Tea rather than 20m. Leave it empty and the preset's own length goes there, as before. This is the plugin's field, not Stream Deck's Title box: the touchscreen layout has nowhere to put a native titlekey draws its whole face as one image, so a native title would land on top of the clock.
