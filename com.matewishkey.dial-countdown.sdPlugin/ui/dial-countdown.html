Tap the screen to pause or resume, tap it twice to reset it to full, hold it to load the next preset without starting it. Press the dial for the next preset, hold it for the previous.

Turn to adjust the clock — never the presets below, which only change here. Once the clock is off its preset the label reads from 20m, and the next press of the dial puts it back.

You step in the largest unit you have already travelled. Move ten seconds and you move in tens of seconds; a minute, and you move in minutes; ten minutes, and in ten-minute steps. Turning back keeps the step but starts the distance over, so hovering never runs away. The step shown on screen says what the next click will do — when it fades, you are back to seconds.
